# Astrology app language and features: SajuTeller benchmark
**Reviewed:** 18 October 2026. This analysis adapts engagement patterns to Korean Saju (Four Pillars), which is not Western zodiac astrology.

| App | Vocabulary and tone | Published feature set | Potential SajuTeller adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Co–Star](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/co-star-personalized-astrology/id1264782561) | Direct, intimate, “Horoscopes from the Void,” “Ask the stars” | Personalized daily horoscopes, friends' chart compatibility, push notifications, chart education | Optional reminders, friend-sharing only with consent, a daily Saju insight; avoid anxiety-inducing assertions |
| [The Pattern](https://www.thepattern.com/app-features) | Reflective and plain-language: “Your Pattern,” “Your Bonds,” “Your Transits,” “Time Travel” | Personality themes, relationship compatibility, cycles and timing, in-depth AI conversation, audio | Date-based Saju themes, connections and reflective prompts, never guaranteed predictions |

## Vocabulary guidelines
- **Discovery:** Discover, Four Pillars, Saju readings, destiny as a *metaphor*, your journey, life's timing, soul connections, daily energy, reader, oracle.
- **Transactions and trust:** Keep unambiguous labels: Request a time, Awaiting confirmation, Session price, Cancel request, Payment unavailable. Do not describe a request as a reservation.
- **Cultural accuracy:** Avoid equating lunar phases, Western planetary transits, zodiac houses or retrogrades with Korean Four Pillars. Adapt familiar interface *patterns*, not unsupported claims.
- **Localization:** Write for Korean, English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and Spanish. Keep booking disclaimers explicit in every language.

## Prioritized feature roadmap
1. **Shipped in this UI branch:** arrows alongside swipeable cards/topic filters/studio chooser; enlarged map and clear names; persistent request CTA and a visible, disabled payment control with an explanation.
2. **Before charging customers:** onboard verified studios and real inventory; add host acceptance, availability and cancellation workflows; select a provider-hosted PCI-compliant checkout; verify payment events with backend webhooks, persist statuses, send receipts and implement refunds.
3. **When core marketplace works:** onboarding by life theme and language, consent-based personal Four Pillars context, optional notes/reflections and daily energy prompts.
4. **Later:** consensual relationship insights, saved/shareable reading summaries, opt-in reminders, short multilingual audio explainers.
